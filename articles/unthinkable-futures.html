The problem with the probable

The essay starts from a simple problem: foresight methods built around continuity are weakest exactly where discontinuity matters most. Probability can organise what is already imaginable, but it cannot fully account for what sits outside the conceptual frame from which those probabilities are constructed.

What are we failing to imagine today?

What changes

The proposed response is not better prediction. It is greater epistemic agility: questioning the frameworks we rely on, using speculative design to stretch the space of the thinkable, and working with AI as a generator of unfamiliar possibilities rather than only as an extrapolation engine.

In other words: train for futures that cannot be derived neatly from the present.

The future is not obliged to fit our models.

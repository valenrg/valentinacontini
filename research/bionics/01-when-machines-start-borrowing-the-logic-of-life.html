The question

What happens when the body itself starts borrowing from biology?

The article follows a progression from imitation to integration and argues that biological embodiment and artificial intelligence need to be mapped on separate axes. A highly capable AI can still inhabit a synthetic body, while a living or biohybrid system may use very little conventional AI.

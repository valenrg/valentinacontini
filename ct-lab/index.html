01 / The lab

A place to try the unreasonable.

I study emerging technologies professionally. Here I get my hands on them. CT LAB is the evolving record of what I build, what surprises me and what survives contact with reality.

Not a product roadmap.

FIELD FILE / 001 / COMPLETED

AURA / ECLIPSE

Technical record for the selected project.

Wearable / EEG-driven generative art / Local-first

One void. One luminous field.

AURA transforms live EEG signals into ECLIPSE: a dark circular void surrounded by an evolving, organic purple corona. The artwork changes geometry, texture, luminosity and movement without claiming to read thoughts or emotions.

01 / Behaviour

Four signals, four artistic dimensions.

Two EEG channels become four continuously varying control values. These are artistic parameters, not measures of cognitive or emotional state.

E / Presence

Energy

Combined RMS amplitude of the filtered channels, logarithmically compressed and adaptively normalized.

EEG amplitude → radius, thickness, luminous presence

Higher values give the corona more visual mass while preserving a minimum visible thickness.

C / Form

Coherence

Pearson correlation between the filtered channels, adaptively normalized. This is an artistic proxy, not a scientific EEG coherence measurement.

Inter-channel correlation → stability and asymmetry

Higher values stabilize the circle; lower values permit organic deformations.

X / Texture

Complexity

Mean normalized spectral entropy across 40 frequency bins from 1 to 40 Hz for both channels.

Spectral entropy → edge detail

The outline gains finer folds, irregularities and local thickness variation within one continuous form.

R / Motion

Rhythm

Magnitude of change in Energy, Coherence and Complexity between successive analysis windows; not a literal neural oscillation rate.

Feature variation → orbit, pulse, drift

Controls a slowly orbiting hotspot, breathing-like expansion and fine surface motion, with damped speed changes.

02 / Architecture

From EEG to light, entirely on the wearable.

  1. Sense + transmitVIE Zone, two live EEG channels. Encrypted, bonded Bluetooth Low Energy connection.
  2. Acquire + checkBLE sample decoding, sample-rate and clipping checks, transient rejection.
  3. Filter + window1–40 Hz bandpass. Overlapping 2-second windows, advanced every second.
  4. Extract featuresRMS amplitude, Pearson correlation, spectral entropy and change between states.
  5. Calibrate + smoothRolling history of 24 accepted windows, percentile normalization and gradual transitions.
  6. Render + displayOrganic corona, 16 radial gradient bands and soft hotspot. Full-screen 16-bit framebuffer sent to AMOLED.
Why local mattersNo laptop, intermediary computer or cloud service is needed for the final EEG-driven artwork. BLE acquisition, filtering, feature mapping and rendering run on the ESP32-S3.
03 / Implementation

Hardware, timing and resilience.

EEG source
VIE Zone wearable, two live channels.
Wearable processor + display
M5Stack StopWatch C152, ESP32-S3 and 466 × 466 circular AMOLED.
Software stack
Arduino/C++, M5Unified, M5GFX and NimBLE-Arduino.
Connectivity
Direct BLE, encrypted/bonded. No continuous computer connection.
Acquisition assumption
Approximately 600 samples/second/channel, based on observed streaming behavior, not independently validated.
Feature cadence
2-second windows / 1-second hop; approximately one artistic feature update per second in normal operation.
Adaptive calibration
24 accepted feature windows in rolling history, roughly 24 seconds under uninterrupted operation.
Rendering goal
30 frames per second target, with a 16-bit off-screen framebuffer to reduce visible flicker.

When EEG disconnects, the system retains its last meaningful artistic state and settles into a quieter autonomous animation. Reconnection runs in a separate processing task; EEG-driven behavior can resume after reacquisition and signal checks.

04 / Visual language

One form, no dashboard.

The continuous organic corona has soft inner and outer edges, dissolving into a true-black AMOLED background. There are no numbers, gauges, icons, labels or decorative particles in the artwork.

05 / Boundaries

Biological input. Artistic output.

AURA is EEG-driven generative art, not a brainwave diagnostic or an emotional-state display. Energy, Coherence, Complexity and Rhythm are names for creative control variables; they do not identify thoughts, attention, relaxation or consciousness.

The IMU/motion-reactive experiment is outside the EEG-only version documented here. The approximately 600 Hz acquisition rate and 30 fps rendering goal are implementation assumptions or targets, not certified performance figures.

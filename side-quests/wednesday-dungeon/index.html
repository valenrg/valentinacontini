Why does this exist?

Because some systems are easier to understand once you turn their incentives, friction and absurdities into mechanics.

Wednesday Dungeon takes a very ordinary return-to-office day and treats it like the system it sometimes feels like: a repeatable quest with resource depletion, random encounters, questionable loot, status effects and a boss fight.

It started as a joke. Then it acquired an inventory system.

What you are up against

  • The commute of destiny and the parking pilgrimage.
  • Desk roulette, coffee crime and premium mediocre lunch.
  • Open-space interruption damage and the plague NPC.
  • Random encounters, loot, status effects and achievements.
  • A final boss powered by the phrase “quickly align.”

No corporations were harmed in the making of this game. Several calendar invitations were.

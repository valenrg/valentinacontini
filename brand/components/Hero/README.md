The page's opening: a text-first split with a kicker, one huge uppercase headline with a single accented phrase, a lead paragraph and an optional side note; from the v1 manual's hero.

The consumer provides: `section.vc-hero` with a `data-surface`, a `.vc-shell.vc-hero-grid` holding the text column (`p.vc-kicker`, `h1` with the accented words in one `em`, `p.vc-hero-lead`) and an optional `aside.vc-hero-aside` (a `.vc-hero-mark`, `.vc-hero-rule`, a `p` with a bold label, and one `TextAction`). The aside drops under the text below `breakpoint-nav`.

- Do accent one short phrase, never more; it should be the idea, not a decoration.
- Do let the headline be the image. No background photo, gradient or illustration behind it.
- Do write the headline as a claim you can stand behind ("I research the future. Then I build bits of it."), not a slogan.
- Do use `.vc-hero-mark` only for a real index, edition or number; leave the aside out rather than fill it.
- Don't use more than one hero per page or a hero inside a section.

An example page showing how the parts compose; copy the structure, not the words.

The structure: an ink `Navigation` bar, a paper `Hero`, then `section.vc-section` blocks that alternate surfaces, each opened by a `SectionLead` and followed by the content in the form that fits it: an `EditorialTile` grid for independent pieces, `FieldFileRow`s for a ruled index, a `Readout` for measured values. Every section sits in a `.vc-shell`.

- Do choose the form from the content: lists for items, tables for comparison, editorial grids for independent pieces, a ruled index for records. Never turn everything into tiles.
- Do alternate `paper` and `ink` sections to mark chapters; two sections of the same surface in a row need a hairline between them.
- Do keep every status and claim true; the example's projects and statuses illustrate the pattern only.
- Don't put a section between the hero and the first `SectionLead` without its own opener.
